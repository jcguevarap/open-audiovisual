Example 28 · Anything can be an instrument範例 28 · 什麼都能當樂器

Jellyfish bloom水母綻放

A world written from one sentence: consonant chords make the swarm bloom, dissonance makes it flinch and go dark.從一句話寫出的世界：協和的和弦讓水母群綻放，不協和讓它們縮起、熄燈。

Try these three things試試這三件事

  1. Play single notes: a jellyfish rises where each pitch sits.
  2. Play a triad: every bell lights up in the root’s colour and the swarm swims in unison.
  3. Play a cluster of adjacent keys: the lights go out and the bells sink, then slowly recover.
  1. 彈單音：每個音高的位置浮起一隻水母。
  2. 彈一個三和弦：每個傘蓋都亮成根音的顏色，整群一起游動。
  3. 彈幾個相鄰的琴鍵（音堆）：燈光熄滅，傘蓋下沉，再慢慢恢復。

No hardware needed. On-screen piano · QWERTY keys · Simulated performer.不需要硬體。螢幕鋼琴、電腦鍵盤、模擬演奏者。
